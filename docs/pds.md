# Product Design Specification — Conqueror Studio Website

Version: 0.1.0 validation release

## Direction

Conqueror Studio is a luxury digital atelier: Renaissance composition, editorial whitespace, and intelligent systems expressed through a calm modern interface.

## Visual tokens

- Background: ivory `#F6F3EE`
- Ink: `#111111`
- Accent: editorial blue `#2046F2`
- Display: Cormorant Garamond-style serif fallback
- Body: Inter-style sans-serif fallback
- Motion: subtle hover lift; reduced motion must be respected

## Responsive frames

- Desktop: 1440px, 12-column editorial grid
- Tablet: 1024px, stacked content where needed
- Mobile: 390px, single column and touch-friendly links

## Homepage sections

1. Navigation
2. Hero / Digital Atelier
3. Philosophy / Manifesto
4. Selected Work / Living Archive
5. Our Craft
6. Latest Updates
7. Journal
8. Contact CTA
9. Footer

## Content truth

Concept projects are allowed only when labelled `Concept` or `Concept archive`. No fabricated client outcomes, testimonials, or social proof may appear.

## Accessibility

- Semantic sections and headings
- Visible focus states
- Meaningful alt text for artwork
- Decorative marks hidden from assistive technology
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
